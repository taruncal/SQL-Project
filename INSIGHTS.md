                                       STRATEGIC REVENUE IMPROVEMENT PLAN                         

1. Boost Average Order Value (AOV) via Combo & Cross-Selling



Observation: 37.99% of orders consist of a single pizza, and the current AOV is $38.31.

Action:

Introduce "Lunch Combos" (1 Small/Medium Pizza + Drink/Side) during peak hours (12 PM–2 PM) to push single-item orders above $25.

Create "Family Meal Bundles" on Sundays (e.g., 2 Large Pizzas + Sides) to drive up order values on the slowest day of the week.







                  
2. Optimize & Trim Low-Performing Menu Items





Observation: The Brie Carré, Green Garden, and Spinach Supreme underperform substantially relative to inventory maintenance costs.

Action:

Remove or rework The Brie Carré Pizza (lowest sales volume at 490 units) to reduce specialized ingredient waste.

Consolidate low-selling veggie options (Green Garden & Spinach Pesto) into a single flagship vegetarian offering.







                                 
3. Implement Time-Based Dynamic Marketing & Promotions






Observation: Demand spikes during 12–2 PM and 5–7 PM, dropping significantly after 8 PM.

Action:

Staffing Alignment: Ensure maximum kitchen throughput during peak 12–2 PM and 5–7 PM windows to avoid lost sales due to prep delays.

Late-Night Incentives: Introduce targeted late-night (8 PM–10 PM) digital discounts to utilize excess oven capacity during off-peak evening hours.

















                     
4. Rationalize Pizza Sizing & Margin Management                                   








Observation: Large pizzas generate 45.89% of revenue, whereas Small and Medium combined represent 52.26%.

Action:

Encourage sizing upgrades by narrowing the price gap between Medium and Large sizes or highlighting "Best Value" badges on Large options in digital and print menus.
